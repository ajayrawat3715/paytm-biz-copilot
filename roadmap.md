# Roadmap

- [ ] Merchant copilot dashboard (warm kirana ledger design): sales snapshot, opportunity cards, at-a-glance strip
- [ ] AI copilot chat (Lovable AI) that knows the shop's numbers
- [ ] Daily bahi-khata metric cards: Total Credit Outstanding; Collections Received Today split into Cash Collections vs Udhar Repayments; Net Daily Balance / Take-Home = (cash sales + udhar repayments today) − daily cash expenses
- [ ] Udhar workflow: create credit sale (customer, phone, items, amount, due date); log partial/full repayments (cash/UPI)
- [ ] Status badges: Settled (balance 0), Overdue (balance > 0 and due date past), Active (balance > 0 and due today/future)
- [ ] Per-customer udhar ledger drawer: full repayment history, remaining balance, inline quick-action repayment modal
- [ ] All summary numbers and statuses update reactively from shared state
